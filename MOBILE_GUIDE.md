# Quick Start Guide - Support Bot Mobile App

## 📱 Install on Your Phone (30 seconds)

### Android Users

1. **Open the app in Chrome/Edge**
   - Tap the menu icon (three dots) ⋮
   - Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"
   - Tap "Install"
   - The app appears on your home screen

2. **Alternative - Blue Banner**
   - Look for the blue "Install Support Bot" banner
   - Tap "Install"
   - Confirm installation

### iPhone/iPad Users

1. **Open in Safari**
   - Navigate to the app URL
   - Tap the Share icon 📤 (bottom center)
   - Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen"
   - Enter a name (or keep default "Support Bot")
   - Tap "Add"
   - The app icon appears on your home screen

## 🚀 First Launch

1. **Tap the app icon** - Opens Support Bot in full screen
2. **See welcome message** - Bot explains what it can help with
3. **Choose a category** or type your question
4. **Get instant answer** - From 96,000+ knowledge base

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### App Not Installing?
- Update your browser
- Use Chrome or Safari (recommended)
- Try HTTPS connection
- Check you have space on phone

### Messages Not Saving?
- Check your internet (if first time)
- Try closing and reopening
- Hard refresh: Pull down to refresh
- Clear app cache in phone settings

### App Crashing?
- Force close and restart
- Clear cache: Phone Settings → Apps → Support Bot
- Reinstall if needed

### Can't Find Home Screen Icon?
- Check another home screen page
- Search for "Support Bot" in app drawer
- Reinstall via browser menu
